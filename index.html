Situation Analysis

Working assumptions: young shoppers in Egypt discover fashion through short-form video, and many want pieces that feel personal. Both should be checked against early results.

Audience segments

Streetwear Enthusiasts

Follow streetwear culture and look for ready-made pieces that match their style.

Customization Seekers

Have their own idea and want a piece designed around it.

SWOT

  • Ready-made and customized options

Weaknesses

  • Limited initial ad budget

Opportunities

  • Self-expression and personalized fashion

Threats

Strategy

Channels

TikTok

Meta Ads

Customer journey

  1. Awareness

    Teasers and launch content reach new people.

  2. Interest

    Customization content shows what is possible.

  3. Purchase

    Customers inquire and order through DMs or the website.

  4. Loyalty

    Keep buyers engaged and share genuine feedback when available.
